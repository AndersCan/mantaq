The viewer's canvas has zoom and pan tools that float at its bottom center: Select (V) and Move (H), Zoom out, the zoom percent, Zoom in and Fit. The tool in use shows as pressed. The percent follows every zoom. Zoom out and Zoom in turn off at 10% and 800%, and Fit turns off while the drawing fits. The canvas keeps room under the drawing for the tools, and while the canvas runs past the bottom of the window, the tools stay at the bottom of the window. Fit and the scroll after a step keep the drawing clear of them. The tools are there in Diagram only too. In full screen the bar with Exit full screen stays at the top while the viewer scrolls.
