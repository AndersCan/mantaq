A second click in the viewer acts, or the element shows as the current choice. A selected state whose Details show, the `?` of the arrow that Details show, and the followed child chip and row are the current choice: they get `aria-current="true"` and have no `role="button"`, no pointer and no tab stop. A followed child no longer has `aria-pressed`. Without a panel, the line under the board names each send of the selected arrow with its step number, so a second send of a loop arrow shows. After a click or Enter sends an arrow that cannot be sent again, focus goes to the first arrow that sends now, or else to the current state.
