The viewer zooms and pans. Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel, and a trackpad pinch, zoom at the pointer. Space with a drag, the middle button, or the Hand tool (H) pan. Ctrl or Cmd with + and - zoom in and out, Shift+1 fits, Shift+0 is 100%, and V picks the Select tool again. The zoom and the tool hold across steps, moves along the path and redraws. The viewer still opens at the fitted width.
