Details of a group node names each arrow end as the canvas does, for example `group payment`, not `@payment`. A move between two states of the group is listed once, under "inside". A state picked in Details of a group node shows the selection ring on its group node. A click on a stub selects that state and opens its Details. Inside an entered group, an arrow whose other branch goes to a state outside the view shows its own condition, not "else".
