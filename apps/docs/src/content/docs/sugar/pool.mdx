## What

`createPool` builds one inbox and `n` workers. Work waits in the inbox, a free worker takes it, and work that fails goes back to the front.

## Why

Hand-rolling a queue with workers means wiring the inbox, the idle and busy tracking and the retry yourself. The pool gives you all three, drawn as parts of your machine.

### Try it

1. Open the run [Four items, three workers](?machine=teamInbox&run=Four%20items%2C%20three%20workers). Three workers take one item each; the fourth, `d`, waits: the inbox box reads `1 waiting` with `d`.
2. In the **Context** tab set `item` to `e` and click the `team.ITEM_ARRIVED` arrow; do the same with `f` and `g`. The box reads `4 waiting`, `d e f g`: the leftmost goes next.
3. Press **advance 1000 ms** in the **Step** tab. The three workers finish together and take `d`, `e` and `f`; the box reads `1 waiting` with `g`.

### Limits

- The queue lives in memory and is lost on restart.
- It has no size limit.
- A failed item is tried again at once, with no limit: resolve in `run` when it should give up.
- Each pool needs its own `name`: the name prefixes every event id, so two pools with one name share ids.
