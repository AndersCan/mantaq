The docs' checkout machine ([`checkout.ts`](https://github.com/anderscan/mantaq/blob/main/apps/docs/src/content/docs/checkout.ts)), drawn at build time from each handler's `to` list. It cannot drift from the code.

## Read it

A box is a state, an arrow is one target of a `to` list labelled with its event. Every other mark is in the [visualizer guide](/mantaq/guides/visualizer/#nodes).

## Walk it

The panel runs the real machine in your browser. Pulsing arrows are the steps you can take next.

1. Click `submitBasicInfo`: the form shows an example payload; press **Send**. The walk moves to `shippingAddress`. The **Context** tab marks the lines that changed.
2. Click `submitShipping` and press **Send**, then the same for `submitPayment`. Effects do not run here: in `submitting` the panel waits for `chargeCard` and offers what it reports.
3. Click `paymentOk` and press **Send**. The walk ends in `success`.

## Parts that talk

A machine whose regions or actor maps send each other events opens on this view. A box is a part, an arrow is an event one part sends and another takes, and the line under the name is where the part is now. A box for an actor map reads `name ×n`, with a count per state. Click an arrow to see who sends it and in which states it is taken, then **Open** to land on that arrow in the part's diagram. **← System** comes back.
