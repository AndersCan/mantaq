## What

A guard decides whether an event may move the machine.

## Why

Some events are only valid under a condition: no plates, no meal. Putting the condition in the handler keeps the rule next to the transition it controls.

## How

<Step n={6}>
  A guard is a plain `if` in the handler: no plates, no `full`. With no plates, `eat` is blocked:
  click `serve` first.
</Step>
