## What

A timer is an event the machine sends itself after a delay.

## Why

Waiting is part of the behaviour: food left on the stove burns. Treating time as an event keeps it in the diagram and testable.

## How

<Step n={8}>
  After 10 seconds in `cooking`, `cookTimer` uses `withTimeout` to send `overcooked`, which moves
  the person back to `hungry` with no plate. An event the machine sends itself is listed under
  `internal`.
</Step>
